Streamer.bot tool to announce yourself and your friends streams on discord

*Inspired by streamcord*

---

**__Features__**
* Use your own `discord bot` or `webhook` to announce your stream on twitch, youtube, kick or trovo! 
* Wanna promote your friends? You can announce your friends aswell!
* Assign a `ISLIVE` role to your friends while being live on stream.
* `@everyone`, `@here` and `@role` mentions.
* With your `discord bot` the script is able to send `Stream Ends` messages so users know if you/your friends are now offline.
* `Crossposts` to other discords if they are subscribed to your announcement channel.
* Dark/Light mode for the GUI
* Your friends can assign themselves to `BsA` through discord commands.
* Offers many features streamcord keeps behind a paywall and more.

Note: This script works best running separately 24/7 on a remote server
