## 📖 Overview

Better Stream Announcements posts go-live and stream-ended embeds to Discord, Stoat or GameVox.
It announces your own channel, the creators you pick as streamer friends and community promotions. It also
runs perfectly 24/7 on a remote server.

## ✨ Highlights

- 📣 **Go-live and stream-ended posts.** Embeds for your own channel, posted to Discord automatically.
- 📡 **Seven platforms.** Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Owncast, Rumble, Picarto and TikTok, with consistent
  announcements across all of them.
- 🎨 **Embeds the way you want them.** Game info, viewer and follower counts, tags, box art, preview images
  and a Watch button.
- 🤝 **Streamer Friends.** It tracks the creators you select and announces their streams too, mapped to
  Discord roles and platform accounts.
- 🔔 **Control over pings.** @everyone, @here or role mentions, automatic crossposting and cooldowns.
- 🙋 **Self-service in Discord.** A bot presence and a self-assign flow let members register themselves for
  announcements and roles.
- ⌨️ **Slash commands.** `/bsa`, `/streamerfriends` and `/crosspost`. `/bsa setup` picks channels, ping roles
  and ISLIVE roles with Discord's own pickers, so nobody has to copy IDs by hand.
- ⚙️ **A settings window.** Configuration, theming and troubleshooting without editing code.
