# Changelog

All notable changes to Better Stream Announcements are documented here.

## v2.1.0 — Stoat & GameVox Copies, a New Settings Window and a Color Picker

### Upgrade notes
- **Nothing to redo.** The four new webhook fields (GameVox and Stoat, for the broadcaster and for Streamer Friends) start empty, and while they are empty everything is posted exactly as before. Import the new version over the old one: ten of the fourteen actions carry new code, and actions, triggers and settings stay as they are.

### Added
- **A copy of every announcement on GameVox and Stoat.** Broadcaster → Platforms and Streamer Friends → Platforms each take an optional GameVox webhook and an optional Stoat webhook. With one set, every announcement also goes to that channel, and the copy follows the Discord message: it is updated with it and switched to its ended form when the stream ends. Your own announcement's copy also comes back with it when the stream returns within the revive window, and a Streamer Friend's copy is deleted together with the Discord message once **Remove Old Message** takes it down. The copy is only ever posted together with a new Discord announcement, so a webhook added while someone is live starts with their next stream. Every call is best effort: a rate limit or a server error is retried once, a failure is logged, and the Discord side carries on either way.
  - **GameVox** speaks Discord's webhook API, so its copy is the Discord post minus what GameVox cannot use: the buttons, the role mentions (those roles only exist on Discord) and the embed timestamp, whose time moves into the footer instead (“LIVE since • 2026-10-25 20:15”). It posts under the webhook's own name and picture.
  - **Stoat** (formerly Revolt) has embeds without an author line, fields or footer, so its copy carries the author line in bold above the embed and the fields and the footer as lines of the description. It has no preview picture: Stoat only takes pictures uploaded through a bot account. Stoat counts the embed text against the message's 2000 characters, so a long description is shortened to fit.
- **Home page** in the Settings window. **Right Now** shows who is live, where announcements go (Discord by bot or webhook, the GameVox and Stoat copies) and how many Streamer Friends each platform has; **Start Here** walks through the setup in five steps, each with a link to its page, and can be hidden (the switch to bring it back is on the About page); **Today** counts the warnings and errors in today's log and names the last problem; **Help And Links** points to the guides.
- **Status Log page** (Help → Status Log): everything BsA wrote to Streamer.bot's log, one day at a time — every line a BsA action logged, plus what Streamer.bot itself reports about a BsA action, such as an error naming it or a sub-action that would not compile. Filter by day, by action, by level and by text (Esc clears the text); **Refresh** reads today's file again while Streamer.bot keeps writing it, and 📁 opens Streamer.bot's log folder.
- **Color picker on every embed color.** Each embed color in the Settings window (Broadcaster Online and Offline, Streamer Friends live and ended) has a swatch beside its hex box that shows the color, and a click on it opens a picker right under the field: a field for saturation and brightness and a hue bar, both to click or drag, sliders for red, green and blue (the mouse wheel nudges them by one), and a row of the colors Discord uses most. Every change goes straight into the hex box, and typing a hex code moves the picker along. Done or a click elsewhere closes it.

### Changed
- **The Settings window is rebuilt around a sidebar.** The nested tabs are gone: a sidebar lists the pages in four groups — Broadcaster, Streamer Friends, Discord Bot and Help — and every page keeps the name it had as a tab, so a hint like “Broadcaster → Platforms” still leads to the same place. The pages open under a colored title band, Home and About under the BsA logo.
  - **Search** over every setting (Ctrl+F): it finds a page by its labels, its checkbox texts and the setting keys.
  - **Unsaved changes** are counted in the footer. **Save** (Ctrl+S) keeps the window open, **Save & Exit** closes it, and closing with unsaved changes asks first.
  - Theme and reset moved into the ☰ menu.

### Fixed
- **Placeholders in Owncast, Picarto, Rumble and TikTok announcements.** The Settings window documents `{user}`, `{title}`, `{game}`, `{url}` and `{viewers}` for Streamer Friends, but these four actions only took `%user%` and the like, so the documented form came out as literal text. Both forms work now. Owncast, Picarto and TikTok report no category, so `{game}` stays empty there.
- **`{game}` on YouTube** comes out empty instead of as literal text, since YouTube gives no category.

### Removed
- The 🎨 button next to **Random Embed Color**. It only opened colorspicker.net in the browser; the picker beside each color replaces it.

## v2.0.1 — Connection-Trouble Notices for Your Own Announcements

### Added
- **Connection-trouble notice for your own announcements.** Broadcaster Online and Offline now count dropouts the way Streamer Friends does: every offline event is a drop, and once a second one lands within 30 minutes the stream is having connection trouble rather than ending. Both embeds say so — the ended one reads **BetterStreamAnnouncements • ⚠️ Connection trouble** instead of **• OFFLINE since**, and so does the announcement that comes back up when the stream returns within the 20-minute revive window, instead of **• LIVE since**. The notice clears itself once the stream has held 15 minutes without dropping again, and a comeback after the revive window posts a fresh announcement with a clean footer. A single dropout never trips it, and two unrelated dropouts hours apart do not add up.
- **“LIVE since” and “OFFLINE since” in the broadcaster footers.** Your own announcements carried a bare **BetterStreamAnnouncements** footer while the Streamer Friends embeds said what their timestamp meant. They now read **• LIVE since** and **• OFFLINE since** like the others. The live one keeps the moment the stream went live even when the announcement is edited later, so an update no longer moves the time to “now”, and the ended one holds the moment the stream dropped even if the first delivery attempt fails and a later one posts instead.

### Changed
- **Every footer separator is • now.** Discord joins a footer and its timestamp with • of its own, so the · the footers used read as a second, smaller separator in the same line (“BetterStreamAnnouncements · LIVE since • 21:34”). All footers use • — both Streamer Friends embeds on all seven platforms, Broadcaster Online and Offline, and the `/bsa setup` page counter.

## v2.0.0 — Slash Commands, `/bsa setup` & Seven Platforms for Members

### Upgrade notes
- **The `?` prefix commands are gone; the bot runs on slash commands now.** `/bsa`, `/streamerfriends` and `/crosspost` replace `?bsahelp`, `?sfadd`, `?cc` and the rest (full list in the FAQ). The bot registers them by itself when it connects. If they don't show up in your server, invite the bot again with the **bot** and **applications.commands** scopes, then use **Slash Commands → Register Again** in Discord Bot → Bot Presence.
- **Message Content Intent is no longer needed** and can be switched off in the Developer Portal. Presence Intent and Server Members Intent stay on; Presence Role Assign needs them.
- **Your trigger bindings keep working.** All custom trigger event ids are unchanged; only their display names now follow the slash commands. The arguments changed a little: Custom Crosspost no longer passes `discordMessageId` / `discordIsDm`, and Self-Assign no longer passes `discord.Message`, `discord.CommandPrefix` or `discord.Arg1…`, since slash commands have no message text. `discord.Command` now holds the subcommand (`link`, `unlink`, …).
- **Removed settings:** `BOT_CommandPrefix`, `SF_WizardTimeout` and `CC_WizardTimeout`. Nothing reads them anymore; they drop out of `settings.json` the next time the Settings window saves. An activity text that's still the old default `?bsaHelp | BsA by aaskjer` becomes `/bsa help | BsA by aaskjer` automatically.
- **"Every Streamer Friend on one platform" crosspost subscriptions no longer receive the broadcaster's own announcement on that platform.** Until now nothing else ever reached them (see *Fixed*), so in practice these subscriptions only ever got broadcaster posts. The broadcaster has its own subscription scope.
- **`/bsa` admin commands with an empty `BOT_AdminUserIds`** used to be open to every member. They now need Discord's **Manage Server** permission, because `/bsa setup` can change the configuration.

### Added
- **Optional display name on YouTube Streamer Friends.** A YouTube channel id reads like `UCnOML5aYvwMOYEGa8ienFEA`, which is what the `/crosspost setup` and `/streamerfriends` pickers had to show members. An entry may now carry a name behind a pipe — `UCnOML5aYvwMOYEGa8ienFEA|Sorylokan` — and the pickers show that instead. Only the part in front of the pipe is the key: it is what gets resolved against YouTube, what the Discord map and the sessions are keyed by, and what crosspost subscriptions match on, so adding a name to an existing entry breaks nothing. The name is display-only, never reaches the embed, and may not contain a comma (that separates entries). `@handles` take a name the same way.
- **Connection-trouble notice in Streamer Friends announcements.** A stream that drops out and comes back twice within 30 minutes is having connection trouble rather than ending, and the live embed’s footer says so: the usual **· LIVE since** is replaced by **· ⚠️ Connection trouble**, so the footer reads **BetterStreamAnnouncements · ⚠️ Connection trouble** while the trouble lasts. The notice goes up during the grace window, so it is there while the stream is actually down, and it clears itself once the stream has held for 15 minutes without dropping again. A single dropout never trips it, and two unrelated dropouts hours apart do not add up. The announcement is otherwise still left untouched for the whole grace window: this notice costs one edit per dropout, not one per poll cycle. All seven platforms.
- **Slash commands** with readable names: `/bsa help | setup | status | refresh-presence | reload-profile`, `/streamerfriends link | unlink | accounts | list | help | service` and `/crosspost setup | list | remove | help | service`. Every answer is private to the member who asked.
- **`/bsa setup`**: picks channels, ping roles, all ISLIVE roles, the presence-check roles, bot admins and the Self-Assign and Crosspost restrictions with Discord's own channel, role and user menus, so nobody needs Developer Mode or copied IDs. Each pick is saved immediately; the Server ID fills itself in when empty. A new trigger, **`/bsa setup (Admin, after each change)`** (`bsaSetupChanged`), fires with the setting and its new value.
- **TikTok as the seventh Streamer Friends platform.** A new action, **[BsA] - Streamer Friends TikTok**, announces a friend's TikTok live, keeps the embed up to date, switches it to its ended form and hands out the ISLIVE role, exactly like the other six. Friends are bare user names (the name from `tiktok.com/@name`), the Discord map separates name and user ID with `:`, and **TikTok Friends**, **Assign Role To**, **ISLIVE Role ID(s)** and **TikTok Embed Color** sit next to Picarto's in the settings window. Members can link their TikTok account with `/streamerfriends link` and subscribe to TikTok friends in Custom Crosspost.

  **TikTok is the one platform here without a public API**, and that is worth knowing before you rely on it. The other six ask a documented endpoint; TikTok has none that answers "is user X live" without a signed request, so this action reads the JSON that the public live page `tiktok.com/@name/live` ships inside its HTML. That is undocumented and can change without warning. Every field is read defensively: an unexpected shape makes that one friend count as not live and never damages the cycle or the stored sessions. Two consequences of the data source are worth knowing: TikTok answers the same way for a name that does not exist as for an account with no live room, so a typo in the list shows up as a friend who is simply never live (the account itself is checked when a member links it through `/streamerfriends link`, which uses the profile page); and the preview image's URL is signed and times out after a few hours, so a long-running announcement can lose its picture. TikTok rooms carry no category, so the game filter and the Category field don't apply to them.
- **Owncast, Rumble and Picarto in Self-Assign and Custom Crosspost.** Members can link accounts on all seven Streamer Friends platforms, and subscribe to them. Every account is checked against the platform before it's saved: Owncast through the server's public `/api/config`, Picarto through its API (same token setup as the Picarto action), Rumble by fetching the livestream API URL.
- **Streamer Friends announcements now reach Custom Crosspost subscribers.** Before, only Broadcaster Online handed its announcements to `[CC] - Crosspost Dispatch`; none of the Streamer Friends actions did, so subscriptions to a platform or to one Streamer Friend never received anything. All seven actions now pass every new live announcement on, without the role mentions, which only mean something on your own server.
- **Slash Commands → Register Again** button (Discord Bot → Bot Presence) for when the commands go missing, for example after inviting the bot again. It needs the **Name** field of the Execute C# Code sub-action inside **[BsA] - Discord Bot Presence** filled in with exactly that text — Streamer.bot's `ExecuteMethod` looks the target up by that Name, not by the action's name, and an empty one can never be found.
- **The Custom Crosspost key is tied to your Windows account.** `BsA_Crosspost.key` used to sit next to the encrypted webhooks in plain base64, so a copy of the `Announcements` folder, from a cloud backup or a support upload, could decrypt every member's webhook. The key is now protected with Windows DPAPI for the account Streamer.bot runs under, and is useless on any other PC or account. Existing key files are converted automatically the first time they're read. DPAPI is called directly from Windows, so no extra assembly reference is needed.
- **Recovery code** (Discord Bot → Custom Crosspost → Webhook Key): **Show** displays the key as a `BSA1-…` code with a checksum, for a password manager; **Restore From Code** puts it back on a new PC or after reinstalling Windows, checking first whether it opens the stored subscriptions. The section also shows whether the key is in order.
- New trigger **`/crosspost setup (subscription saved)`** (`bsa_cc_saved`) with the subscription's id, platform and streamer.
- `/streamerfriends unlink` without options lists your linked accounts on every platform to pick from; `/crosspost list` comes with a menu to remove one.

### Changed
- **Embed footers read “BetterStreamAnnouncements”** instead of “BsA by aaskjer”, everywhere an embed carries one: both Streamer Friends embeds on all seven platforms, Broadcaster Online and Offline, Custom Crosspost and the Discord Bot embeds. Owncast, Rumble, Picarto and TikTok also switch their footer separator from “|” to “·”, so every platform looks the same. The bot’s activity text is a setting rather than a footer and is left alone.
- **Server ID moved from Streamer Friends to Discord Bot → Bot Presence**, under the bot token. It stopped being Streamer Friends configuration some time ago: the role lookups, `/bsa setup` and the scope its slash commands are registered to all read it. The Streamer Friends tab points to its new place. Nothing changes in `settings.json`.
- **Both wizards run on buttons, menus and pop-up forms** instead of emoji reactions, typed replies and DMs. The crosspost webhook URL goes into a private form, so members don't need DMs from the bot open. The wizards keep their state in the components themselves: nothing times out, and there are no leftover messages to clean up.
- **The bot asks Discord for fewer events.** Slash commands need no intent, so the bot no longer receives every message and reaction on the server; it only listens for interactions, members and presences.
- Role checks for Self-Assign and Crosspost use the roles Discord sends with each command, instead of a member cache built from gateway events plus an API call per unknown member.
- Every Discord gateway event used to load `settings.json` in Self-Assign and Crosspost, presence updates included. Both now ignore everything except their own interactions before touching the disk.
- Reworked the English texts in Discord replies, the Settings window and the FAQ for clarity and a more natural tone. The repeated Developer Mode hint is a shared line now, and points to `/bsa setup` where the field can be picked there.

### Fixed
- **A Twitch announcement lost its preview image a few minutes after the stream ended**, turning into Twitch's grey "no preview" placeholder while the embed still said LIVE — through the whole reconnect window, until the announcement was switched to its ended form or deleted. The action never touched the embed during that window, and that was exactly the problem: Twitch drops the preview the moment a stream ends (the URL redirects to `ttv-static/404_preview`), Discord's image proxy re-resolves the hotlinked URL on its own schedule because Twitch serves previews with `max-age=300`, and from then on it serves the placeholder. Not editing cannot prevent that. The last good frame is now pulled back out of Discord's proxy as soon as the stream goes offline and re-uploaded as an attachment on the announcement, which Discord hosts itself and never re-resolves, so it stays exactly as it was until the ended embed replaces it or the message is deleted. The embed is reused verbatim — only the image source changes — and the frame is checked against the real placeholder first, so a preview that was already gone is never frozen in its place. A rate-limited edit (code `30046`) keeps the frame and retries on the next cycle.
- **A stream could hang as "live" forever — never marked as ended, never deleted.** When a Streamer Friend went offline, the action first rewrote the announcement into its "ended" form and only closed the session once that edit had gone through. An edit that could never succeed therefore pinned the session in the reconnect window for good: the ISLIVE role stayed on, the embed kept showing the stream as running, and **Delete Ended Announcements After**, whose timer only starts once the session is closed, never began counting. Two things set this off:
  - **Changing the bot token** while announcements were still up. Discord does not let a bot edit a message another bot posted (`403`, code `50005`), so every announcement from the previous token was stuck for good.
  - **Discord's rate limit on editing messages older than an hour** (code `30046`). With **Update Announcements** on, the viewer-count refreshes of everyone still live share that quota with the end-of-stream edits, and on a longer friends list they used it up before the cycle reached the friends who had just gone offline — every cycle, indefinitely.

  Each friend now gets a limited time to land that edit: 10 minutes after a hard rejection, 45 minutes while rate limited. After that the session is closed anyway, so the role comes off and the announcement is deleted on schedule. Friends who are offline are also handled before the ones still live, so the end-of-stream edits get the quota first instead of last. An announcement posted under an older bot token still can't be edited, but it can be deleted, as long as the current bot has **Manage Messages** in the channel.
- **Owncast and Rumble role maps could not be saved correctly from the Settings window.** Their actions read `url|DiscordId` (a URL already contains `:`), but the window only accepted `name:DiscordId`, so every working entry was rejected and every accepted one didn't work. The check now uses `|` for those two, and the tooltips show the right format.
- **Saving the Settings window undid changes made from Discord in the meantime**, such as accounts linked with `/streamerfriends`, `service on/off` or `/bsa setup` picks. Saving now writes only the fields edited in the window on top of the current file.
- **Any member allowed to link themselves could link accounts to other members** with the old admin syntax (`?sfadd <discordId> …`). Acting for someone else is now limited to the Self-Assign admin roles, as the settings always described it.
- **A crosspost subscription removed while a delivery was running could come back.** The delivery saved its whole copy of the subscription list afterwards. It now applies only its failure counters to a freshly loaded list.
- **Triggering the Bot Presence action while it was running reconnected the bot.** `StartPresence` tore the live session down before checking whether one was already running, so a trigger bound to the action itself (like the help or bot-info event) dropped and rebuilt the gateway connection every time. A running session is now left alone.
- **Owncast, Rumble and Picarto never revived an announcement.** **Session Cooldown (Hours)** is the window in which a friend who goes live again gets their "ended" announcement turned back into a live one. Twitch, YouTube and Kick skip that window only when it's set to `0`; the three newer actions had the test inverted, so with the default of 1 hour every ended stream was reset at once and a quick return always posted a fresh announcement. The tooltip and FAQ also described the setting as a safety net for stuck sessions; both now say what it does.
- **Picarto preferred the manual bearer token over Client ID + Secret.** The settings describe the bearer token as the fallback, used only while Client ID or Client Secret is empty, and that's how the Picarto action and Self-Assign pick the token now. With both set, the self-renewing token is used, so a forgotten manual token no longer stops announcements once it expires.
- **A role ID in "Admin User (IDs)" locked everyone out of the `/bsa` admin commands.** The field only compared user IDs, while every other admin setting in BsA takes roles. It's now **Admin User/Role (IDs)** and accepts both, mixed: a member counts as bot admin when their own ID or one of their roles is listed. The `/bsa setup` picker for it takes members and roles as well.
- **A missing or unreadable crosspost key wiped the stored webhooks.** When decrypting failed, the next save of the subscription list (any new subscription, removal or failure counter) overwrote the stored ciphertext with an empty value. If the key file was missing altogether, the action also quietly created a new one. Locked subscriptions now keep their ciphertext, are skipped during delivery instead of being counted as failed (so their owners don't get a false "your webhook keeps failing" DM), and new subscriptions are refused until the key is restored.
- **`/bsa status` reported a heartbeat latency up to a whole interval too high.** When Discord asks for an immediate heartbeat (opcode 1) instead of waiting for the next scheduled one, BsA sent it but didn't record when - so the acknowledgement was timed against the *previous* heartbeat, and a missing acknowledgement for it never tripped the zombie-connection check either. Both are now handled the same way as a scheduled heartbeat.
- **`/bsa status` showed `n/a` as the heartbeat latency for the first minute after a connect**, which reads like something is broken. The first heartbeat is deliberately delayed by a random 0 to 41 seconds, as Discord asks, so there is genuinely nothing measured yet; it now says *measuring…* until the first answer arrives and keeps `n/a` for when one is actually overdue.
- **The "Slash Commands" row label was unreadable in dark mode.** Every label added next to a button kept WPF's near-black default instead of the window's theme color.
- The `sf_help` and `sf_toggle` triggers were registered but never fired. They fire now.
- Several tooltips said things the code doesn't do: Crosspost admins can't *add* subscriptions for others (only remove), and the Twitch Client Secret steps ended with "copy Client ID".

### Removed
- The prefix command parser, the emoji-reaction wizards with their session tracking, timeouts and message cleanup, and the DM fallbacks. Custom Crosspost dropped from 1862 to about 1540 lines, Self-Assign from 1924 to about 1450.
- Unused code: the OAuth paste dialog, `AddMultilineWithInfoLink` and `FindCheckBoxByTag` in the Settings window, and `ToUnixSeconds` in the Twitch, YouTube and Kick actions.

## v1.6.1 — Bot Presence Reliability & Profile Colors

### Upgrade notes
- **Two new assembly references are required:** `PresentationCore` and `WindowsBase`, on the **Streamer Friends (Twitch/YouTube/Kick)** and **Broadcaster Online** sub-actions. They decode profile pictures for the new color options below — without them those actions won't compile. References are declared per sub-action in Streamer.bot, so each one has to be ticked individually.

### Fixed
- **The bot could go offline and never come back.** Several paths in Discord Bot Presence ended in a session that stayed dead until Streamer.bot itself was restarted — in the worst cases without a single error line to explain it:
  - **Rejected privileged intents (close code `4014`) were treated as fatal.** BsA always asked for Presence, Server Members and Message Content, and Discord refuses the connection outright if the application isn't approved for one of them — which happens when the intents get switched off in the Developer Portal, or automatically once an unverified bot passes 100 servers. The session was marked fatal, and the watchdog is explicitly not allowed to restart a fatal session, so the bot simply stayed offline. It now reconnects without the privileged intents so the bot comes online and the presence works, and says loudly — in the log and in `?botinfo` — that Presence Role Assign and the Discord chat commands are degraded until the intents come back.
  - **A dying session could kill the one replacing it.** Shutdown gives the old connection loop three seconds to notice it was cancelled, so it can still be unwinding while a new session starts. If it recorded a fatal error on the way out, that error landed on the *new* session, which then quit on its first iteration and was never restarted. Fatal errors are now stamped with the session that hit them and ignored by any other.
  - **A dead settings watcher was never noticed.** Live settings updates ride on a `FileSystemWatcher`, which Windows stops permanently — without an error anyone could see — when its buffer overflows or the watched folder is replaced. From that point the presence never changed again no matter what was saved. The watcher now has an error handler, is rebuilt on every reconnect, and the settings are re-read directly on every connect so a missed file event can't outlive the reconnect.
  - **Every reconnect burned a wasted round trip.** When resuming had already been ruled out, the session id was left in place, so the reconnect still sent a `RESUME` that Discord could only answer with "invalid session". Conversely, a reconnect that *could* have resumed closed the socket with code `1000`, which tells Discord to throw the session away. Both are now consistent with what the reconnect is actually doing.
- **"Streaming" activity with no usable Stream URL showed no activity at all.** Discord silently drops a Streaming activity unless it carries a `twitch.tv` or `youtube.com` link, leaving the bot online with a blank activity line — which reads exactly like the presence being broken. It now falls back to *Playing* and logs why, matching what the setting's tooltip already promised.

### Added
- **Set Bot Presence** (`BOT_PresenceEnabled`, Discord Bot → Bot Presence) — controls whether BsA sets the bot's status and activity at all. With it off the bot still connects and every command and feature keeps working; it just shows as plain *Online* with no activity, and the rest of the Bot Presence section is ignored. Turning it off on a running bot clears the activity right away instead of leaving the last one stuck. Useful when something else already drives the bot's presence, or to rule the presence out while troubleshooting.
- **Use Streamer's Profile Color** (`SF_UseStreamerColor`) and **Use My Profile Color** (`BA_UseStreamerColor`) — matches the live embed's side bar to the streamer's own channel color instead of a fixed per-platform hex.
  - On **Twitch**, this uses the broadcaster's chat name color, read from Helix `chat/color`. It's the only real per-user color any of the three platforms exposes through an API, and it needs no extra scope — the existing app token covers it.
  - On **YouTube** and **Kick** — and for Twitch users who never picked a chat color — the color is derived from the dominant hue of the profile picture. Pixels are weighted by saturation, so a small vivid logo beats a large muted background; greyscale avatars produce a matching grey instead of an arbitrary hue.
  - Falls back to **Random Embed Color** (if enabled) and then to the configured per-platform hex whenever no color can be determined, so existing setups are unaffected while the option is off.
  - Resolved colors are cached in memory per streamer and per avatar URL, so a friend costs one lookup rather than one per poll cycle.
  - **Kick caveat:** Kick serves avatars as WebP, which Windows can only decode when the optional *WebP Image Extensions* component is installed. Without it, Kick friends fall back to the configured color and the log notes `avatar format not supported by Windows imaging`.

### Changed
- **`?botinfo` now reports what actually matters when something is wrong.** Alongside connection, uptime, last READY and latency, it shows whether the presence is switched on, whether live settings reloading is still working, whether the privileged intents were rejected, and any error that stopped the session. Previously it could show a healthy-looking embed for a bot that was going nowhere.
- `?refreshpresence` now says so plainly when the presence is switched off, instead of reporting a successful refresh that did nothing.

## v1.5.0 — ISLIVE Role Conflict Fix & Settings GUI Reliability Pass

### Fixed
- **Failed to compile on Streamer.bot 1.0.4 stable** (`CS0012: The type 'IDynamicMetaObjectProvider' is defined in an assembly that is not referenced`) in `bsa-discord-bot-presence.cs`, `bsa-custom-crosspost.cs`, and `bsa-self-role-assign.cs`. All three built Discord embed field arrays using `new JArray { ... }` (C#'s collection-initializer syntax), which requires the compiler to verify `JArray` implements `IEnumerable` — pulling in `System.Core` for `IDynamicMetaObjectProvider`, a reference Streamer.bot 1.0.4's compiler doesn't provide by default. Switched every occurrence to the equivalent constructor-call form, `new JArray(...)`, which sidesteps that check entirely.
- **The Settings window could crash Streamer.bot entirely.** Closing and reopening Settings, then toggling "Show Debug Sections," could throw a cross-thread WPF exception and take the whole process down, requiring a restart. The debug-section list is now reset on every window build instead of accumulating stale references from previous sessions. `Show Debug Sections` also now resets to off when the window is actually closed (matching the existing rainbow-mode behavior), while still surviving a theme-switch reopen.
- **Presence Role Assign was fighting Streamer Friends over the ISLIVE role.** If a role was configured in both the Discord-presence-based checker and Streamer Friends' Twitch/YouTube/Kick API checker, the presence checker could strip a role the API check had just (correctly) granted — most visibly when a member doesn't use Discord's own "Streaming" activity, or Discord is slow to report it. Presence Role Assign now only reacts on an actual live→offline transition it observed itself, and only ever removes roles it granted in the first place — roles from other sources are never touched, no matter how long presence stays "not live."
- Added a short grace period before Presence Role Assign revokes a role after detecting an offline transition, to absorb brief gaps/jitter in Discord's presence data.
- The Streamer Friends "Offline" tab's **Random Embed Color** checkbox was bound to the Broadcaster's setting instead of its own (`SF_RndEmbedColorEnded`) — toggling it changed the wrong feature, and the setting that actually controls the ended-embed color could never be turned on from the GUI.
- **Add VOD/Channel Button** (Broadcaster → Offline) incorrectly rendered a color-picker button next to it, left over from a copy-pasted control.
- Removed a stray, unrelated line of text in the Custom Crosspost panel that also caused a layout row collision with another info line.
- **Toggle Broadcaster Online Cooldown** (debug section) was bound to a settings field the Broadcaster Online script never actually read, so it silently did nothing. Now correctly wired to the field the script uses.
- `Profile Banner Url` and `Profile Description` in Discord Bot → Bot Presence were saved but never applied to the bot's Discord profile. They now actually update the bot's banner and "About Me" text (on startup, via the `!bsaReloadProfile` command, and automatically when settings.json changes).
- Corrected several settings tooltips that referenced chat commands that don't exist (e.g. `sfashelp`, `ccashelp`) with the real ones (`sfadd`/`sfremove`/`sfmy`/`sflist`/`sfhelp`, `cc`/`cchelp`).

### Added
- **Skip Offline/Invisible Discord Users** option for both Presence Role Assign (`PS_SkipOfflineUsers`) and Streamer Friends (`SF_SkipOfflineUsers`), for servers that don't want to flag a friend as live if they're intentionally set to Offline/Invisible on Discord.
- Presence Role Assign now publishes each tracked member's Discord status so the Streamer Friends scripts can honor the option above (requires Presence Role Assign to be enabled).
- **Update Announcements** (`BA_UpdateAnnouncements`) for Broadcaster Online — edits the live announcement in place as your title, category, or viewer count changes, instead of it staying frozen at whatever it looked like when you first went live. Works in both bot mode and webhook mode.
- **Webhook message editing** for Broadcaster Online and Offline — both can now edit their own messages when posting via a webhook, not just with a bot token. Broadcaster Offline's "mark the announcement as ended" works the same way over a webhook as it already did with a bot.
- **Revive window** for Broadcaster Online — if your stream comes back online shortly after being marked ended, the existing announcement is edited back to live instead of a new one being posted.
- **Native Kick box art** for Broadcaster Online and Offline — category artwork is now pulled directly from Kick's own API, the same source Streamer Friends' Kick integration already used, instead of depending on a SteamGridDB API key. SteamGridDB remains available as a fallback for platforms with no native art (YouTube).

### Changed
- Renamed the unlabeled "BOT_PresenceUrl" field to "Stream URL" in the settings GUI.
- Reviewed and rewrote settings tooltips across every panel — clarified additive vs. override behavior between "All Platforms" and per-platform ISLIVE roles, documented `{user}/{title}/{game}/{url}/{viewers}` placeholder support for Streamer Friends messages, added missing dependency notes (e.g. options that only apply while another checkbox is enabled), and fixed assorted typos/grammar.
